# Anahita page structure

Source: supplied Adobe XD export folder `Anahita Website Struktur` (nine artboards). Its grayscale boxes describe layout; the separate Anahita style guide controls colors, fonts, logos and icons.

| Artboard | Route | Section order |
|---|---|---|
| Startseite | / | Wide banner, centered introduction, projects, image/text with help link, reversed news feature, two support cards, FAQ, sponsors |
| Projekt | /projekte/[slug] | Compact banner, intro, three statistics, centered team portraits/contact, location and schedule, contact section, FAQ, project sponsors |
| News | /news/[slug] | Compact banner, title, article, three related articles |
| News_Archiv | /news | News grid, project grid |
| Mitglied Werden | /mitglied-werden | Banner, introduction/steps, application/contact cards, donation link, FAQ |
| Über uns | /ueber-uns | Banner, introduction, statistics, team grid, founding content, image/text sections, support cards, FAQ |
| Stellenangebote | /mitmachen | Banner, expectations/statistics, job cards, application form area, FAQ |
| Hilfe_Beratung | /beratung-hilfe | Immediate exit/help controls, banner, introduction, contact options, team, information sections, FAQ |
| Spenden | /spenden | Banner, introduction, bank/PayPal columns and amount selector, receipt information/contact, membership link, FAQ |

Content stays in Sanity. Team email links use published team profiles. Project FAQ and sponsors have dedicated Studio fields. Other pages use their existing sections, actions, statistics, teamMembers and faqs fields. Homepage support cards always link to membership and donations; uploaded CMS images take precedence.

Unpublished pages remain unpublished. No invented job benefits, legal guarantees, help numbers, sponsor affiliations or download documents are supplied. Missing editorial details show honest empty states. The application form is visibly disabled until the submission backend is implemented. The existing local contact form remains a preview. PayPal still requires the organization's supplied URL, and transfer details still require its account-holder name.

The footer remains static with the approved five-column layout. Existing Anahita colors/fonts and circular portraits remain.
